About BULLY OFF

A field-hockey coach/manager game built on a deterministic match engine. Everything in it is fictional and everything runs on your device.

Privacy

  • No real clubs and no real people. Clubs, players, coaches and towns are generated from common-name pools and checked against a list of real clubs; no person is modelled, rated or named.
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising. The app makes no network requests after it has loaded; your careers live in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) and in the files you export yourself.

Offline

Installed as an app, BULLY OFF works without a connection: the engine, the world generator and your saves are local.
