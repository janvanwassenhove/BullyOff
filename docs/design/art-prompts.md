# Art prompts — the generated imagery (ADR-012)

Every shipped image in `apps/manager/public/{title,intro,onboarding,portraits}/` was generated with ChatGPT (image model, 2026-08-22) from the prompts below, then fitted to the exact size the component expects with `tools/art/fit.py` (cover-crop around a focus point + Lanczos resize → webp) or `tools/art/portraits.py` (3 × 2 sheet → six 192 × 224 portraits). Nothing is traced from a real photograph, club or person; every prompt forbids text, logos, badges and recognisable faces.

Shared art direction (from the design handoff): dark ink background (`#06080a`–`#0b1014`), desaturated dark teal, one emerald accent (`#1f9a63`), cream linework (`#e9e4d8`) for the illustrated cards, floodlit and freshly watered turf for the cinematic pieces, painterly rather than photographic.

## Intro film stills (Phase 10.1)

`intro/film-{1..6}.webp`, generated at 1536 × 1024 and fitted to 1600 × 900 (focus centre, quality 80). Every prompt starts with *"Create a wide landscape image (1536x1024). Frame N of a cinematic intro sequence for a field hockey manager video game:"* and ends with the shared style line *"Painterly digital illustration with photographic lighting, deep shadows, desaturated teal palette with one emerald green accent (#1f9a63). No recognisable faces, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no badges, plain unbranded kit."* The scene lines:

1. early morning at a small amateur hockey club, the empty blue-green artificial turf pitch being watered by sprinklers, fine spray catching low golden sunlight through mist, a few hockey sticks leaning against the pitch fence in the foreground, a modest clubhouse with a small empty stand behind, floodlight masts switched off against a pale dawn sky; no people.
2. inside a modest amateur club dressing room before a match, seen from the doorway: matching dark teal shirts on hooks along a wooden bench, sticks, shin guards and turf shoes lined up below, an open kit bag, a small magnetic tactics board leaning against the wall, a bare bulb and cold daylight from a high window, steam from the showers; no people.
3. the warm-up under floodlights at dusk, players in matching dark teal kit jogging in a line across a freshly watered turf, sprinklers still throwing spray at the far side, low camera angle close to the turf, motion blur on the nearest legs and sticks, floodlights just switched on; players from behind or in silhouette.
4. a penalty corner at night under floodlights, seen from behind and beside the goal at a low angle: four defenders with face masks and the keeper on the line, the injector at the backline pushing the ball out, the ball skimming across the wet surface with a spray trail towards a drag-flicker loading up at the top of the circle.
5. half-time on the team bench at night, light rain: a coach in a rain jacket and beanie crouches in front of the seated substitutes with a small magnetic tactics board, players lean in with sticks between their knees, steam rising, water bottles on the wet ground, the glistening turf and a floodlight mast behind; seen from behind the players.
6. the final whistle at night under floodlights: players in dark teal kit seen from behind in silhouette, sticks raised, running towards their bench on glistening watered turf, spray kicked up from their feet, the ball in the back of the net on the left, a few raised arms in the small stand in the distance.

To regenerate an asset: paste the prompt, download the PNG, then

```bash
python tools/art/fit.py source.png apps/manager/public/intro/poster.webp 1440 560 --focus 0.5,0.4
```

```bash
python tools/art/portraits.py sheet.png m apps/manager/public/portraits
```

Not generated: key-moment thumbnails in the post-match report (rendered from the replay at the moment's tick), club crests (procedural, `ui/Crest.vue`), the PWA icons (`tools/icons/make-icons.cjs`). The intro film (`intro/film.mp4`) is still optional; the poster plays with a push-in until one exists.
